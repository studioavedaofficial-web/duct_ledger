Inside the workspace

From the first measurement to the final BOQ.

Start with the drawing

Upload a multi-page PDF, name the sheets and calibrate each one against a known length. Verify a second dimension before tracing. Flag a mixed-scale sheet to prevent measurements at the wrong scale.

Zoom, pan or rotate the drawing. Snap to available endpoints, lock a run horizontally or vertically, and undo or redo an edit.

Explore the sample workspace ↗

Duct runs & fittings

Trace rectangular and circular ducts, enter a vertical rise or place an elbow, tee, reducer, damper or diffuser. Fittings use whole-piece counts.

Choose Save & measure next to reuse dimensions, system, waste and BOQ mapping. Each pair of endpoints creates a separate duct.

Lengths & areas

Measure a straight length, follow a polyline or trace a polygon. Add cutouts to see gross, deducted and net area separately from duct surface area.

Select a rectangle and review similar outlines on the sheet. Save the selected areas together and rescan without doubling the total.

Reviewed AutoCount

Box one diffuser or grille and search the sheet for similar symbols. Click to reject false matches or mark a missed symbol yourself.

Accepted locations become individual drawing items. A repeat search skips locations already counted.

Rates & presets

Import contractor rates from CSV or Excel, map the columns and check units. Choose or add a compatible rate directly in the drawing inspector.

Save recurring duct and fitting settings as presets. Review material waste, optional labour and the effect of changing a shared rate.

Bill of quantities

Group measured items by BOQ code. Expand a line to inspect its source items, measured and priced quantities, rounding and amount. Roll compatible contractor codes into one client line and see when a group uses mixed rates.

Populate a copy of the client’s Excel BOQ while preserving its formulas and unrelated cells. Preview the destination cells before downloading.

Revisions & approvals

Freeze the approved baseline, attach a revised PDF and pair its pages, even when the drawing order changes. Align the overlay, adjust opacity or compare sheets side by side.

Review additions, removals and resized items by sheet. Inspect old and new dimensions, quantities and rates before confirming a change.

Queries & drawing evidence

Pin a tender question to its location on a sheet, then record the response in the query register. Follow a changed item from its report back to the drawing.

Variations & reports

See scope and rate effects, credits, and pending or confirmed priced subtotals. A missing rate leaves the overall total incomplete while known money stays visible.

Export an Excel workbook, download a CSV table or print a client report with drawing evidence. Outstanding checks block final issue while draft reports remain available.

Local projects & backups

Open a fresh sample or start your own tender. Drawings, rates and revisions stay in this browser. Once the workspace has been cached, you can reopen it offline.

Download a project backup, restore it on another device or delete an individual project from this browser.
